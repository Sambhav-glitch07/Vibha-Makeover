Booking & Client Policy.

At Vibha Makeover, each booking date is reserved for a specific client. These guidelines explain how confirmations, cancellations, rescheduling and event-day arrangements work. Please read them before confirming your booking.

01 / BOOKING

Confirmation & Advance Token

  • Advance requirement: A 40% advance of the total package amount is required to confirm the slot.
  • No holding slots: Dates are not held on verbal commitments or enquiries. A date is considered reserved only after payment confirmation.
  • Payment modes: The advance may be paid through UPI, net banking or against a cash receipt.
02 / CANCELLATION

Cancellation & Refund Terms

  • Non-refundable advance: If a booking is cancelled 30 days before the wedding/event date or at any time thereafter, the advance amount will not be refunded.
  • Emergency / unforeseen circumstances: In the event of a family medical emergency or natural calamity, the advance may be adjusted against another family event within the following six months instead of being forfeited.
  • Short-notice cancellation: If cancellation occurs less than 15 days before the event, compensation equal to 50% of the total estimated bill is payable.
03 / DATE CHANGES & EVENT DAY

Rescheduling, Final Payment & Venue Setup

  • Rescheduling: A booking can be moved to a new date only if the Vibha Makeover team is available. If the team is unavailable on the new date, the original advance is considered expired.
  • Remaining balance: The remaining 60% payment must be cleared on the event day before makeup begins.
  • Venue setup: The client must provide sufficient white lighting near the makeup chair, an AC/fan and continuous power plug points.
